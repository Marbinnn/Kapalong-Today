PUBLIC SERVICE • OCTOBER 22, 2026

National ID services reach Barangay Gupitan

A National ID mobile registration activity was held in Barangay Gupitan, Kapalong, on October 22, 2026, during a Barangay Information Caravan initiated by the Philippine Information Agency Region XI.

The Philippine Statistics Authority’s Davao del Norte Provincial Statistical Office joined the activity to bring registration services closer to residents.

According to the PSA report, twenty people were registered for the National ID during the activity.

The team also issued sixty printable ePhilIDs to beneficiaries who needed accessible proof of identity.

The activity showed how agencies can work together to make public services easier to reach, especially in communities with limited access to government offices.

Residents should follow official PSA announcements for information about future registration schedules and the documents required.

The report was published by the Philippine Statistics Authority on October 27, 2026, and readers can visit the source for the complete official release.

COMMUNITY SERVICE • OCTOBER 19, 2026

Kapalong police launch a Handog Tsinelas activity for children

The Kapalong Municipal Police Station held a Handog Tsinelas activity for children in Barangay Tiburcia during the celebration of Araw Tiburcia on October 19, 2026.

A report published by PS Balita said the program was supported by police personnel and community stakeholders.

The activity provided children with new slippers for their everyday needs.

The report identified Police Major Raul M. Lucas, officer-in-charge of Kapalong MPS, among those involved in supporting the program.

The initiative was described as part of efforts to promote children’s welfare and strengthen relationships between police and the community.

Community programs like this can be most useful when organizers coordinate with local leaders and make sure assistance reaches the intended beneficiaries.

This article is based on the published report, and readers can follow the source link for the original details.
