LOCAL DESTINATION • SUA-ON

Okbot Cave and the natural attractions of Suaon

Okbot Cave is one of the natural attractions associated with Barangay Suaon in Kapalong, Davao del Norte.

The cave is known for its limestone formations and passages that make it interesting to visitors who enjoy nature and exploration.

Cave environments are delicate, so visitors should avoid damaging rock formations or leaving rubbish behind.

Anyone planning a visit should check local guidance, weather conditions, and whether an accredited guide is required.

Proper footwear, suitable lights, and attention to safety instructions are important when exploring underground spaces.

Responsible tourism can create opportunities for local guides while helping protect the natural site for future visitors.

With careful planning and respect for the environment, attractions like Okbot Cave can help more people appreciate Kapalong’s natural landscape.

AGRICULTURE • KCAST CAPUNGAGAN

KCAST brings weather monitoring closer to local agriculture

An automated weather station was installed at the proposed AgriTourism Center of Kapalong College of Agriculture, Sciences and Technology in Barangay Capungagan.

A SunStar Davao report said the installation was completed on August 25, 2026, through Project SARAI.

Weather data can help farmers understand local conditions that affect planting, crop care, and harvest planning.

Monitoring rainfall, temperature, and other weather indicators may support better-informed agricultural decisions.

Students can also learn how environmental information is gathered and used in real projects.

The equipment is most useful when data is maintained and made understandable to the people who can apply it.

Connecting education, technology, and farming is one way a local college can contribute to practical community development.
