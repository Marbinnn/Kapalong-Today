BASKETBALL • KAPALONG

Kapalong basketball connections reach the professional game

A February 2026 sports report highlighted basketball players Dave Supnet, Romy Ang, and Dodong Postanes in a story connected to Kapalong.

Their names bring attention to the local basketball talent and the communities that help young players develop.

Athletes often build their skills through school competition, community games, regular practice, and coaching.

Support from families, teammates, mentors, and local fans can help players stay committed to the sport.

Good sports development also requires safe places to train and opportunities for young players to compete.

Local sports coverage should accurately report players’ teams, achievements, and current status rather than rely on rumor.

Stories about players with Kapalong roots can inspire younger athletes while recognizing the hard work behind every opportunity.

REGIONAL SPORTS • DAVRAA 2026

DAVRAA 2026 gives young athletes across the region a stage

The 2026 Davao Regional Athletic Association Meet opened in Tagum City on February 23, bringing student-athletes from across the region together.

A SunStar Davao report stated that 9,801 delegates represented eleven delegations at the event.

Regional athletic meets give students a chance to compete, represent their schools, and learn from other athletes.

Training for a major competition takes teamwork among athletes, coaches, school staff, and families.

The event also gives communities a chance to recognize young people’s effort and discipline.

For local athletes, competitions like DAVRAA can be valuable steps in developing confidence and experience.

Results and names should always be checked against official organizers before being shared as final standings.
